Technology 1 · Computer Vision

Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG)

A software-driven computer-vision technology that turns standard digital cameras into health sensors. MediaPipe Face Mesh maps hundreds of facial landmarks, zeroing in on the ROI around the eyes, eyelids and forehead where skin is thin and dense with superficial capillaries — then tracks invisible “micro-blushes”: subtle variations in green-light absorption caused by blood pulsing through the vessels with every heartbeat.

01

Landmark mapping

MediaPipe Face Mesh locks 478 facial landmarks per frame and isolates the ROIs: forehead, eyelids, periorbital and cheek zones where capillaries sit closest to the surface.

02

Micro-blush extraction

Mean green-light absorption is sampled from those ROIs 30× per second. Blood volume pulses imprint a tiny periodic wave on the green channel — invisible to the eye, obvious to the math.

03

Spectral decoding

The signal is detrended, band-passed and transformed (FFT). The dominant frequency becomes BPM, beat intervals become HRV, and the pulse amplitude plus low-frequency modulation give a perfusion index and an experimental breathing estimate.

What this build actually measures

♥ Heart Rate (BPM)

Dominant frequency of the green-channel pulse wave, refined by parabolic interpolation of the FFT peak. Accurate to roughly ±3 BPM under good, diffuse light.

⌁ Heart Rate Variability (RMSSD)

Beat-to-beat intervals are accumulated for 60 seconds before a value is shown. A 10-second window does not contain enough beats to mean anything.

≈ Respiratory Rate

Experimental: breathing modulates pulse amplitude and baseline. Reported when the 0.1–0.6 Hz band has a clear peak.

◉ Pulse Amplitude & Perfusion Index

Direct measurements of the pulsatile component of reflected green light. Unitless — useful for tracking your own signal over time, not for comparing people.

⊘ SpO₂ and Blood Pressure

Not measured by this site. Pulse oximetry needs red and infrared wavelengths; cuffless BP needs per-person calibration. An RGB webcam cannot provide either, so no number is shown.

◐ Autonomic Load

A derived 0–100 index built from heart rate, HRV and breathing. It is a wellness heuristic, not a stress diagnosis or a psychiatric measure.

Technology 2 · Iris biometrics

Your iris is a verifiable key — not a medical oracle

An iris photograph honestly gives you two things: a biometric template that can prove identity with very low error rates, and a set of observable iris features (colour, pupil size, crypts, pigment spots). It does not read your genes or diagnose disease. This module shows exactly what is measured, what is inferred, and what needs a clinician. Retinal (fundus) analysis is deliberately not performed here — it needs a fundus camera and a validated model.

What an iris photo cannot tell you

  • Your genes. Iris colour is ~98% heritable (largely HERC2/OCA2), but this measures the phenotype, not the genotype. Carrier status and polygenic risk need a DNA test.
  • Specific diseases. Diabetes, kidney disease, Alzheimer's and Parkinson's are not diagnosable from an iris photograph. Iridology has failed controlled testing.
  • Neurology. Retinal nerve-fibre thinning is a real research marker, but it requires OCT imaging — not an iris selfie.
  • Emotion, lies or substance use. No validated iris-photo method exists for any of these.

The only genuinely strong claim here is identity: iris texture is stable across life and carries enough entropy for biometric matching. Even that degrades with contact lenses, glare, eyelashes and low resolution.

How the iris code works

01

Localise & unwrap

MediaPipe iris landmarks from the 478-point face mesh give the pupil centre and limbus. The iris ring is resampled into a 512 × 64 polar “rubber sheet”, so pupil dilation and head tilt stop mattering.

02

Gabor phase code

Concentric ring structure is removed, local contrast is normalised, then a bank of log-Gabor filters at 3 scales × 6 orientations encodes phase rather than brightness. Each filter pair contributes two bits — the same principle as Daugman's iris code.

03

Masked Hamming match

Eyelash and glare samples are masked out. Matching searches ±11° of rotation and ±1 radial band, then reports the Hamming distance: below 0.35 suggests the same iris, above 0.45 suggests a different one, and the band between is inconclusive.

Iris identity

Implemented

Stable texture, high entropy, and a rotation-tolerant Hamming match. This is the one strong claim on the page — and even iris biometrics can be defeated by a good presentation attack.

Chronological age

Not implemented

Retinal “age gap” models exist, but they need fundus photographs and validated training data. An iris selfie is not an age sensor.

Biological sex

Not implemented

Retinal models report roughly 80–90% AUC in research. That is a population statistic — not a reliable measurement of one person, and not available here.

Ancestry

Not supported

Iris colour correlates weakly with ancestry. Inferring ancestry from pigmentation is not a genetic test and has a long history of misuse.

Smoking

Research only

Retinal vessel calibre changes are associated with smoking in large studies. There is no validated iris-photo equivalent, so this build makes no claim.

Sleep debt

Not supported

rPPG can show autonomic arousal; it cannot quantify how much you slept. Sleep assessment needs history, actigraphy or polysomnography.

Alcohol / substances

Not supported

Pupil size reacts to light, focus and arousal. A photograph cannot establish intoxication, and treating it as if it could would be harmful.

Fitness

Not supported

Resting heart rate is one weak input. A 60-second webcam clip is not a fitness test, and nothing here estimates VO₂ max.

Stress & anxiety

Partial, non-specific

HRV reflects autonomic arousal, which correlates with stress — but also with caffeine, posture, breathing and illness. It cannot diagnose anxiety.

Lie detection

Not supported

Polygraph-style methods are unreliable, and no eye-photo method is validated for deception. This site does not attempt it.

Mood & depression

Not supported

Facial capillaries do not encode mood. Depression is a clinical diagnosis made from history and validated instruments.

Autonomic tone

Measured

RMSSD is computed over 60 seconds of beat intervals. It describes beat-to-beat variability — not your private emotional state.

Evidence

What eye research can actually predict

These are real research directions, mostly from retinal imaging. None of them run in this build, and none of them are an individual diagnosis.

⧗ Retinal age gap

Fundus-based deep-learning models estimate a “retinal age”. In large cohorts a gap of 3+ years has been associated with higher all-cause mortality — typically hazard ratios around 1.5–1.7, not a 49–67% individual risk. It needs a clinical fundus camera and a validated model.

⌁ Retinal vessel geometry

Arterio-venous ratio and vessel tortuosity correlate with cardiovascular risk in epidemiology. QUARTZ is a research model computed from fundus images — not a timeline for a fatal stroke.

🧠 OCT & neurodegeneration

Peripapillary retinal nerve-fibre layer thinning is measurable with OCT and is being studied in MS, Alzheimer's and Parkinson's. An iris photograph cannot see the RNFL, so this site makes no neurological prediction.

The honest version

Why there is no “death date” here

A percentage attached to a single person implies a precision the underlying cohort data does not have. Risk models produce population-level associations; a clinician combines them with history, examination and validated tests. A browser demo cannot substitute for that, and pretending otherwise is how people get hurt.

⚠ What a real system would require

A clinically validated imaging device, a model trained and calibrated on a representative cohort, regulatory clearance, and a clinician to interpret the result alongside the rest of your history. None of that exists in a web page.

✔ What this build does instead

It measures what a camera can actually measure — pulse, pulse variability, breathing, iris colour and iris texture — and tells you the error bars. Where a number cannot be trusted, it shows “not measured” instead of inventing one.

Identity

Iris identity: strong, not absolute

Iris texture is one of the better biometrics we have — stable across life, high entropy, and hard to duplicate by accident. It is also irrevocable, spoofable with effort, and regulated as sensitive data.

🪪 Proof of personhood · World ID

Specialised hardware such as “The Orb” captures high-resolution iris and facial-depth data to issue a cryptographic proof of humanness. It combines biometrics with liveness checks and a proprietary fraud pipeline — not a mathematical guarantee that no one can ever be spoofed.

👁 Device authentication · Optic ID

Commercial systems such as Apple's Optic ID match iris patterns under infrared light on-device. Apple reports separation between identical twins; the template stays in the Secure Enclave and never leaves the device.

🔐 Iris template

Your iris pattern is stable, but unlike a password it cannot be rotated if leaked. That is why this build stores a template only with explicit consent, only in this browser, and gives you a Forget button.

The privacy question

Should you be concerned?

Yes. A camera pointed at your face produces sensitive signals, and an iris template is permanent in a way a password is not. Here is the honest risk surface, and exactly what this build does about it.

⚠ Where the risk comes from

  • Biometric templates are irrevocable — you cannot rotate your iris if a template leaks.
  • Pulse and breathing patterns are health data, even when the diagnosis is wrong.
  • Cloud scans become training data, breach data and subpoena data.
  • Any system that claims to read mood, lies or disease from a photo is making claims it cannot support.

✔ How AI Eye Scan answers it

  • 100% on-device: every frame and pixel is processed in this browser tab.
  • No upload: there is no backend, no analytics call and no image storage.
  • No defaults: the camera starts only when you press the button, and stops on Esc.
  • Opt-in template: the iris code is written to localStorage only if you tick the box — and Forget removes it.
  • No invented traits: outputs that cannot be measured are shown as “not measured”, not as numbers.

Anything scanning in the cloud should be asked one question: “Who else can see this, and forever?”

Questions

FAQ

Is AI Eye Scan a medical device?

No. It is an educational, on-device demonstration of rPPG and iris biometrics. Nothing here diagnoses, treats or prevents any condition. Heart rate, HRV, breathing and iris measurements are estimates with real error bars, and the ocular signs are screening prompts that require a clinician to interpret.

How accurate is webcam rPPG?

Heart rate from the green channel is typically within a few BPM of a chest strap under good, diffuse light with a still subject. HRV needs a longer window, so it stays hidden until 20 beat intervals have accumulated (about 30–60 seconds). SpO₂ and blood pressure are not measured — an RGB webcam does not have the wavelengths or calibration for them, so this site shows no number.

Does any of my data leave my device?

No. MediaPipe Face Mesh is bundled with this site and runs locally in WebAssembly; all frame pixels and computations stay in your browser. The site has no backend. An iris template is stored in localStorage only if you explicitly tick the consent box, and the Forget button deletes it.

What is the difference between the two technologies?

rPPG reads the present: a pulse waveform from tiny colour changes in your skin. Iris biometrics read identity: a stable texture code that can be matched later, plus observable features such as colour and pupil size. Neither one reads your future.

Can an iris photo reveal my genes or medical conditions?

No. Iris colour is strongly heritable (mainly HERC2/OCA2), but a photo measures the phenotype, not the genotype — it cannot report carrier status or polygenic risk. A small number of conditions produce visible iris signs (Wilson's Kayser–Fleischer ring, NF1 Lisch nodules, Down syndrome Brushfield spots), but those are slit-lamp findings. A phone photo can only raise a “possible — confirm clinically” flag, never a diagnosis. Iridology is not supported by evidence.

What hardware would real retinal analysis need?

A fundus camera or OCT scanner, a validated deep-learning model and a clinician to interpret it. This site deliberately does not pretend a webcam or an uploaded photo can replace that.
